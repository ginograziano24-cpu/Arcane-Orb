Arcane Orb: opening intro (work in progress)

Double-click Intro.html to watch it in your browser (Chrome or Edge recommended).
- Click "Sound on" for the music and sound effects.
- Hold the mouse on the picture to skip to the title.
- The style buttons switch the hand-drawn half between pencil, ink & watercolour, comic and flat cartoon (flat is the default).

The game itself does not play the intro yet: it is a preview for feedback.
