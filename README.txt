ARCANE ORB  -  build 1.0 RC
=====================================

Run ArcaneOrb.exe. No install needed. Starts fullscreen at your
monitor's native resolution (F11 toggles windowed). Renders at native
resolution (1440p / 4K) and runs at your monitor's refresh rate.

CONTROLS
  Mouse ............ move paddle        A/D or Arrows ... move paddle
  Click / Space .... launch orb, fire   Shift ........... faster keyboard move
  Esc / P .......... pause              F11 ............. fullscreen
  Right-click / E .. class ability (Pyromancer, Stormcaller)
  1 / 2 / 3 ........ read a scroll      Hold R .......... restart the run
  Every key and pad button can be remapped in Settings > Controls.

  CONTROLLER (Xbox / PlayStation / Steam Deck)
  Left stick / D-pad  move paddle      A ............... launch orb
  X / RB / RT ....... class ability    Start ........... pause
  In menus: stick or D-pad to move, A to choose, B to go back,
  LB / RB to turn pages and switch Codex tabs. Keyboard menus work the
  same way with arrows / WASD, Enter or Space, Esc, and Q / E.

DUELS  -  1v1 with a friend
  - Main menu > Duel a Friend. Best of three races through the same
    seeded chambers with the same origin (the host picks) and an
    identical item kit that grows each round. No lives or health: a
    dropped orb or a crushing costs you time while the paddle reforms
  - Watch your rival's board live on the right, and both progress bars
    under the chamber title
  - Connect through Steam (6-letter room code or the open-room list, no
    port forwarding), on your LAN (rooms appear automatically), or
    directly over the internet with a room code (host forwards TCP 7777)
  - See PLAYTEST.txt for the friends build

ORIGIN MASTERY  -  levels 1 to 10 for every origin
  - Each origin earns its own mastery XP from your runs (the same XP
    that raises your Arcane Rank). See it under the origin list
  - Rewards are options, not raw power: 2 Livery (paddle colours),
    3 a signature item joins that origin's pool, 4 Orb Trail colour,
    5 an Aspect (an alternate passive, e.g. Bloodlust: faster Rage with
    a lower cap), 6 Starting Gift (begin each world holding the
    signature item), 7 a free shop reroll, 8 silver frame, 9 +5% damage,
    10 gold frame and the title of Master
  - Livery, Orb Trail, Aspect and Gift are toggles on the origin screen
  - Duels ignore mastery so both players stay even

LOOKS AND FEEL
  - Every world has new set dressing that reacts to play: scarecrows
    spin and crows scatter when the orb hits their wall, braziers and
    lanterns light as the chamber is won, the Ruins gate opens and the
    Clouds rainbow appears on the final wave, equalizers pulse with
    combos, steam vents puff, gauges climb
  - Screen shake now builds with the size of the hit and rolls the
    camera slightly; bricks squash when struck; the combo counter pops
  - The orb is always the brightest thing on the board and keeps an
    outline on bright floors and behind tall stacks
  - Larger HUD labels; the advance gauge counts down its last seconds

NEW IN THIS BUILD  -  longer chambers, three fresh worlds
  - Every world is a fresh start: items, relics, fusions, scrolls and
    gold reset when you reach a new world, and you climb again from
    your Sanctum gifts and starting gold. Lives and health refill
  - Chambers come in waves (elites in three). When a wave is nearly
    gone the stragglers crumble and the next formation drops in
  - The formation advances a row whenever the ADVANCE gauge under the
    chamber title fills; bricks that reach the red line smash for damage
  - Heat: one point per item (two per fusion) you hold in this world.
    Each point adds 7% brick health, more armoured bricks and a quicker
    advance, and Guardians scale with it too, so late chambers stay
    real fights
  - Rune caches: most waves hide one. Catch it and the fight pauses
    while you choose one of three items (or take 10 gold)
  - Balance (autopilot, 4 origins): about 95-145 s per chamber from the
    first to the last of a world, 160 s for elites, 90-105 s per
    Guardian, where late chambers used to fall in 15-30 s

POLISH PASS
  - Save & quit: from the pause menu or the map. Continue on the main
    menu resumes the exact moment (a chamber restarts from its start,
    same layout). The run file syncs with Steam Cloud
  - Run recap: where your damage came from, the run's key moments act
    by act, your build, unlocks, and Play again. The Codex keeps your
    last 40 runs in a new Run History tab
  - The last brick of a chamber breaks in slow motion as the camera
    leans in; crits land with a tiny freeze
  - New item, Crescendo: combo hits ring a second note and show damage
    numbers that grow and warm with the combo (tuned against Combo
    Engine: a little stronger early, weaker late)
  - Hover any card to see what its keywords mean; first-time tips walk
    a new player through the map, chambers, rewards, shops and more
  - Settings > Controls: remap keys and pad buttons. Settings > Access:
    text size, game speed (slowed Endless runs stay off the global
    board), colour filters for protanopia / deuteranopia / tritanopia,
    tips on / off. Display: fullscreen, borderless or windowed; the HUD
    spreads to the edges on ultrawide screens. Effects: fast animations
  - Hold R in a chamber to restart; the pause screen shows your damage
    so far
  - Steam: friends see what you're playing (class, act, world), the
    Deck's keyboard opens for seed entry, and a crash writes crash.log
    next to your save

NEW IN 1.0 RC  -  readability, Guardians, controllers, Steam
  - Special bricks carry clear floating badges (anvil = armoured, bomb =
    explosive, bolt = chain, heart = regenerating, shield, swirl =
    teleport, star = multiplier, gem = crystal, "?" = mystery, crown =
    elite ...) so you can read a chamber at a glance
  - Bricks break with a flash, a ring on the floor and tumbling shards;
    big bricks and elites land with a brief hit-stop and a shockwave
  - Guardian intros: the camera starts low beside the arena while the
    Guardian assembles row by row, its name rises over a turning magic
    circle, and its eyes ignite (click to skip). The Guardians are now
    sculpted: a domed head with glowing rune seams and a crown of
    crystal spikes
  - Illustrated How to Play: seven pages covering controls, the map,
    every brick type (with its badge), power-ups, items and fusions,
    the origins, and the meta game
  - Full keyboard and controller navigation in every menu, with a
    glowing focus ring and controller button prompts
  - 33 achievements with an in-game unlock toast and a new Codex tab
    (progress bars for counted goals); they sync to Steam when the game
    runs through Steam
  - Steam support: achievements, cloud saves (Auto-Cloud), Steam Deck
    detection (controller prompts by default) - see STEAM.md
  - Store kit in store/: trailer, screenshots, capsule art and
    achievement icons

NEW IN 0.6  -  story, endless mode and classes
  - Story Mode: the three acts are now the main story, with a prologue,
    chapter cards between acts and an epilogue after the Crystal Warden
    (click to reveal, Esc to skip)
  - Endless Mode: beat the story once to unlock it from the main menu.
    The worlds loop forever with generated levels; every loop bricks get
    25% tougher, orbs 6% faster and dropped orbs hurt more. Each loop
    is worth 2,500 bonus score. You can also keep going straight from
    the victory screen.
  - Endless Records: a local top-10 leaderboard (Codex > Endless Records)
  - Two new classes with an active ability on Right-click / E:
      * Pyromancer: every 4th hit ignites, fire burns 50% hotter.
        FIRESTORM sets the two lowest rows ablaze (22s cooldown)
      * Stormcaller: every paddle bounce arcs lightning, lightning +30%.
        THUNDERSTRIKE smites the toughest brick (or the boss core) and
        chains to 4 more (18s cooldown)
    Ability medallion with cooldown ring on the HUD; new sounds for both
  - Class select redesigned to fit all 7 paddles

WHAT'S IN THIS BUILD
  - Procedural level generator: 16 base formations, combinable, symmetric
    or asymmetric, with reachability checks. Every level shows its seed.
  - 15 brick types: basic/reinforced, steel, armoured, explosive, chain,
    split, regenerating, moving, shield, teleporting, spawner, multiplier,
    crystal, mystery, elite
  - Roguelite run: 3 acts with branching maps (combat, elite, shop,
    treasure, event, rest, boss), 28 upgrades + 30 relics across 6
    rarities, 12 synergies (own 3 items with the same tag)
  - Three act bosses: Prism Colossus, Crucible Golem, Crystal Warden
  - 5 paddle archetypes (2 unlockable), Sanctum meta-progression (shards),
    seeded runs, daily challenge, combo system, procedural music that
    intensifies with your combo
  - Settings: volumes, screen shake, flashes, particles, VSync, FPS
    counter, bloom and colour grading
