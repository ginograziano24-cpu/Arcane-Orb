# Arcane Orb — future updates

Ideas for later updates (not scheduled yet).

## Content
- **More items**, kept balanced: check each new item with the campaign sims before it ships.
- **Unique bosses** that do more than throw projectiles.

## Story and character
- **Origin lore** that is more immersive.
- **General world lore** that is immersive but simple to understand.
- **Origin quips**: short lines when an origin is selected, and when high-value things happen in a run.
- **Origin intro/showcase**: a brief intro for each origin, possibly a quick in-game demo of their abilities, so players grasp what they do right away.

## Modes and screens
- **Cameo challenges** at chamber select: special challenges that let the player try a different character.
- **Chamber select redesign**: simpler but charming; the current screen looks a bit stale.
- **Multiplayer stage select.**
- **Soundtrack selection**: name the new tracks and add them all to a music picker.

## Collectibles
- **Origin art** unlocked by completing challenge chambers: designer-style images of the origins, e.g. cute scenes of them hanging out or dueling.
