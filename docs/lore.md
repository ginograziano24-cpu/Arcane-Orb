# Arcane Orb: lore (draft 2, simple)

Tone: a warm storybook with corny jokes. One idea a player gets in five
seconds: **bad guys built walls, heroes smash them with a magic ball.**

---

## The story in one breath

Three giant bosses are burying the world in bricks. Every day the walls get
taller and the sky gets darker.

There is one thing that can break them: **the Orb**, a ball of pure magic.
Whatever it hits, it smashes.

Seven unlikely heroes have teamed up to use it. They take turns carrying the
Orb into the walls, one hero at a time, until all three bosses fall.

That's it.

---

## The three bosses

- **The Prism Colossus** — a giant made of coloured glass. It walls up the
  bright lands: Neon Grove, Sunset Meadow, the Ruins and the Cloud Kingdom.
  Act I.
- **The Crucible Golem** — a huge iron worker in the Machine Works, forging
  the bricks for everyone else. It has never taken a day off and is proud of
  it. Act II.
- **The Crystal Warden** — the boss of the bosses, high in the Crystal
  Caverns. Its walls are the toughest of all. Act III.

---

## The seven heroes

Names are optional; the origin names in game stay the same.

**Guardian (Oriel).** A knight who swore to protect people, and walls are
getting in the way of that. Catches the Orb dead centre to send out holy
light. The responsible one. Keeps everyone's bedtimes.

**Berserker (Brakka).** A wall got built through her house while she was
napping. Now she hits every brick she sees, harder and harder. Loud, fast,
secretly the nicest one.

**Arcanist (Vell).** A wizard who couldn't pick a favourite element, so his
Orb cycles through all four. Reads spellbooks to the group at camp. Nobody
asked.

**Engineer (Rivet).** Grew up in the Machine Works and built her own laser
paddle. Hits hardest when she holds still and thinks. The smart one.

**Gambler (Sol).** The Orb landed on his card table mid-game, so he counts it
as part of his winning hand. Gets stronger the more gold he carries. Owes
everyone money.

**Pyromancer (Cinder).** Loves fire more than anything. Sets bricks alight and
makes them burn hotter. Says she's in it for the explosions. She is.

**Stormcaller (Zephyr).** Misses seeing the sky, so can't sit still. Keep the
paddle moving to build static, then the Orb turns into a lightning storm.
Best friends with the Engineer, the one hero who loves standing still.

## The story cards (replaces the current ones in story.go)

**PROLOGUE — The Orb**
- Three giant bosses are burying the world in bricks. Every day, the sky gets
  a little darker.
- But there is one thing no wall can stop: the Orb. Whatever it hits, it
  breaks.
- Seven heroes have taken up the Orb. Today, it's your turn.
- Smash the walls. Beat the bosses. Bring back the sky.

**CHAPTER II — Into the Machine**
- The Prism Colossus shatters into a rain of coloured glass. For the first
  time in years, you can see the stars.
- But someone is still making the bricks. Deep in the Machine Works, the
  Crucible Golem keeps the forges burning.
- Go down there. Put out the fire.

**CHAPTER III — The Top of the Wall**
- The forges go cold. The hammers stop.
- High above, in the Crystal Caverns, the last boss is waiting: the Crystal
  Warden, the toughest of them all.
- Climb.

**EPILOGUE — The Sky Is Back**
- The Crystal Warden cracks, and every wall in the world crumbles with it.
  Sunlight pours back in.
- The heroes are tired, a bit singed, and very proud.
- Then, somewhere, a single brick wiggles... Looks like the Orb isn't done
  yet.
