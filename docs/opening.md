# Arcane Orb: opening cinematic (draft 2)

About 55 seconds, before the first run. Skippable, plays once on first
launch, rewatchable from the Codex. Preview: the "Arcane Orb Opening"
artifact. The wizard is **Archmage Fumbleton**.

**Look.** First half: a graphic artist's sketchbook. Taped notebook page on a
desk, coffee ring, pencil lines that "boil" like hand-drawn animation,
hatching, coloured-pencil washes that soak in after the line art,
handwritten margin notes with arrows, page turns between shots, slow camera
drift. Second half: arcane. Layered nebula, god rays, rising rune glyphs,
counter-rotating magic circles, bloom, film grain.

| Time | Shot | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Fumbleton's crooked tower and its mess are drawn in; notes "Archmage Fumbleton (very old)" and "important sock" (setting up the trip) | This is Archmage Fumbleton, the oldest wizard at the Arcane Academy. His tower is a disaster. |
| 0:06 | He raises his wand: "Tidy... Up!" He steps on the sock and topples face-first; UP is crossed out and a red **OUT!** slams in; the spell zigzags out of the window ("whoosh"); dizzy stars, "oops." | One morning he tried a Tidy Up spell. Halfway through, he tripped over a sock. |
| 0:13 | Page turn. Outside: the spell arcs from the tiny tower into a POOF cloud (screen shake); the Prism Colossus (rainbow bands), Crucible Golem and Crystal Warden appear holding a duster, a mop and a dustpan, shouting "TIDY!" | It came out as Tidy OUT. The spell shot out of the window, and out popped three enormous bosses. |
| 0:20 | Page turn. Hills, river, trees, birds and sun are coloured in, then a brick wall drops in row by row and swallows the sun; "even the SKY?!" | They tidied everything outside into bricks. The hills, the rivers, even the sky. |
| 0:26 | The Orb falls with a motion trail; white flash and shake; the paper scorches and burns away in a ring of fire, embers and charred paper flakes flying, revealing the magic behind | So Fumbleton fetched the one thing that can break any spell. |
| 0:31 | The Orb bounces off a glowing paddle and smashes rune-stone bricks: hit-stop, shockwave, flash and debris on every hit, sparkle trail | The Orb: a ball of pure magic that smashes whatever it hits. Mostly bricks. |

| 0:47 | The Orb slams down, a shockwave, the logo forms letter by letter with a glint; tagline; Fumbleton pops up in pencil saying "sorry!" | Now it's your turn. Smash the walls, beat the bosses, and bring back the sky. |

Building it into the game: the preview is canvas drawing, which maps
fairly directly onto Ebitengine (vector paths, the HUD portraits, our own
glow). Plan it as its own roadmap item after the user signs off.
