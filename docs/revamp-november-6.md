# November 6 development changes

Seven diversified heroes are integrated across game selection portraits, masks, effect anchors, all 28 HUD poses, editable Blender source, GLBs and both intro previews. Different builds, faces, hair and equipment silhouettes distinguish the classes. Six visible faces now have determined, hurt and smile morphs; the Guardian keeps her visor. Native game captures verify selection framing and HUD crops. This is a stylized development pass, not a completed realistic character production pipeline.

The chamber screen now projects a spell chart from an orb, with engraved rings, distinct combat/elite/guardian sigils and reachable glowing seals. Its attunement panel displays the real chamber reward and omen. Existing graph geometry, reachability, save data and entry rules are retained. Native captures cover three acts at 1280×800, 1600×900 and 1920×1080. Rendering did not mutate the graph. Navigation focus and confirmation entered reachable rooms in all three acts; physical controller hardware remains untested.

Intro work adds actual scenery contraction into a forming brick, articulated receiving arms and fingers, hand-following orbs and subtle facial reactions in the Blender preview. Hidden Boolean cutters and volume-only helper meshes no longer export as visible geometry. All 12 skinned GLBs pass loader/weight checks. Seven exported wrist locations match Blender source landmarks to within 0.000001 world unit. The scene-logic check covers 830 timestamps and all seven hand catches, using real Three scene code with a renderer stub; it does not verify GPU output.

The illustrated preview was rendered at 21 representative times with native Skia. Three monster-transformation options and a 13-panel call-to-action board are delivered separately; planned gestures are clearly identified as plans. The separate intro previews retain guide narration, warm/panic/heroic score sections and independent audio sliders. They are not integrated as native-game playback yet. Browser playback, actual audio playback and sustained 60 fps remain unverified because permitted local browser access was unavailable. CPU render timings do not establish browser frame rate.
