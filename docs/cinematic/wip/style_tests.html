Arcane Orb: the opening

Archmage Fumbleton needs his tower clean before his girlfriend arrives, so he buys an Instant Tidy scroll. The bosses it summons tidy the whole world into bricks. A sketchbook cartoon until the Orb burns through the page, then the seven heroes march out of a summoning circle.
